Murmuration

Thousands of starlings, moving as one. No leader. Three small rules.

Move your cursor or finger through the flock.

Change the rules, change the flock.

Every bird follows the same three habits. Adjust how strongly each one pulls.

Each bird watches only about seven neighbours.

That is enough. A turn that starts at the edge of the flock crosses it in a fraction of a second, and the whole cloud bends together.
